# PremiumTV 1.1.6 — easier app navigation

Build target: Android 1.1.6 (versionCode 101060000) and macOS 1.1.6.

- Open the app to a list of categories. Pick one to browse its channels; use
  All channels, library-wide search, or the back button to navigate elsewhere.
- Channel cards continue to use their published logos, with category artwork as
  a fallback when a logo is missing or cannot load.
- Optional first-run setup saves a viewer name locally. Existing favorites and
  recently watched remain intact. This is a foundation for profiles, not a
  multi-profile or cloud-sync feature.
- Refreshed launcher art uses a TV without text, including the Android TV
  launcher banner.
- The web edition is unchanged.
