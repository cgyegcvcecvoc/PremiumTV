# PremiumTV 1.1.4 — full-screen player decluttered (stable patch)

Prepared 2026-10-31. Android `1.1.4` / `versionCode 101040000`, macOS `1.1.4`.

It installs straight over 1.1.3, 1.1.2, 1.1.1, 1.1.0 and every earlier build — no uninstall needed — and Settings → App updates offers it to every build below it.

## Full-screen player

- **Bottom overlay buttons removed.** The buttons that ran across the bottom of a full-screen stream — Favorite, Side player, Close and the server hop — are gone. They covered score bugs and other on-screen graphics in football and other live sports, and they could not be clicked by remote anyway. Press **Back** to tuck the stream into the side player (it keeps playing while you browse) or close it back to the channel browser. The player's own controls — Mute/Unmute and the Audio track switcher — stay in the top corner, and the player's built-in controls appear on interaction and fade away on their own.

Everything else from 1.1.3 is unchanged: broadcast audio recovery, Movies & Shows, built-in transport controls, automatic server backup and the docked side player.
