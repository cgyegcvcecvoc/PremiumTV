# PremiumTV 1.1.19 — Local channels by location

Android `1.1.19` / versionCode `101190000` · macOS `1.1.19`.

- A location fix reports every published market in reach, nearest first, with its distance.
- Metro clusters such as Dallas / Fort Worth are offered as alternatives, not just the nearest market.
- City entry matches the place, not the spelling: Saint Paul and St. Paul both work.
- The Linux desktop edition ships in the same release: AppImage, .deb and tar.xz.
- Android, macOS and web share the same area rule.
