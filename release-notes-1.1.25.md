# PremiumTV 1.1.25 — Movies tab retired

Android `1.1.25` / versionCode `101250000` · macOS `1.1.25`.

- The Movies & Shows tab is gone from the web player, macOS and Android.
- Four tabs remain: Channels, Live Events, TV guide and Settings.
- The on-demand catalogue stays bundled; live playback and its transport are unchanged.
